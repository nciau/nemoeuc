Leatt Pivoting Knee & Shin Guards

[01]

Daily wear on top of jeans, stashed in the backpack otherwise. Adjust once and they hold — no constant re-strapping. Started on V2s which wore down after 1–2 years, upgraded to V3s. When my wheel cut out, these took zero knee damage. Strap fabric frays over time, but that's honest wear.

Bell Super Air R MIPS Helmet

[02]

Two-plus years of daily riding. It took real impact when my wheel cut out — the hit split between my wrists and head, and the MIPS did its job. Still wearing it. If you're on an EUC without at minimum a solid half-shell with MIPS, you're borrowing time.

BOSONER Wrist Guards

[03]

Worn every single ride. Stretchy, quick on and off, no wrestling with velcro. Loose enough at the wrist to still use a Pebble smartwatch underneath for music and notifications. No knuckle or finger coverage, but they've held up and they're only $14.
