# NemoEUC — Claude Code Build Brief

## What this is
An Amazon affiliate gear review website for EUC (electric unicycle) riders. Pure EUC niche — no general products. The site owner (Nemo) rides a Veteran Sherman L daily and reviews only gear he actually uses. Traffic will come from TikTok/Instagram/YouTube (@notthefishnemo).

## Site name & branding
- **Name:** NemoEUC
- **Handle:** @notthefishnemo (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages (free) at nemoeuc.github.io
- **Domain:** None yet — start on GitHub Pages subdomain

## Design direction
- Nemo will provide a reference website link for animation/UX direction — wait for that before finalizing the visual design
- Current palette: dark void (#0A0A0B) with safety orange (#FF5A1F) accent — works well, keep it
- Typography: Space Grotesk (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (labels/metadata)
- Logo icon: EUC wheel mark (spoked circle), NOT a fish
- Scroll-reveal animations on sections and cards
- Exploded EUC technical diagram in hero area (SVG, animated parts floating independently with dashed leader lines to numbered labels)
- Hero features the actual Sherman L photo (front-oblique) on the right half
- Scrollable photo gallery strip of the Sherman L from 7 angles below the hero
- Amazon affiliate disclosure bar at top (required by Amazon TOS)

## PRODUCT DATA — PROTECTIVE GEAR (card layout with images)

### 1. Leatt Pivoting Knee & Shin Guards
- **Link:** https://amzn.to/451We0a
- **Badge:** CRASH TESTED
- **Review:** Daily wear on top of jeans, stashed in the backpack otherwise. Adjust once and they hold — no constant re-strapping. Started on V2s which wore down after 1–2 years, upgraded to V3s. When my wheel cut out, these took zero knee damage. Strap fabric frays over time, but that's honest wear.
- **Stats:** Worn: 2+ yrs | Crashed: Yes | Fit: Set once

### 2. Bell Super Air R MIPS Helmet
- **Link:** https://amzn.to/4wAvb8f
- **Badge:** CRASH TESTED
- **Review:** Two-plus years of daily riding. It took real impact when my wheel cut out — the hit split between my wrists and head, and the MIPS did its job. Still wearing it. If you're on an EUC without at minimum a solid half-shell with MIPS, you're borrowing time.
- **Stats:** Worn: 2+ yrs | Crashed: Yes | MIPS: Yes

### 3. BOSONER Wrist Guards
- **Link:** https://amzn.to/3S6hjna
- **Badge:** DAILY DRIVER
- **Review:** Worn every single ride. Stretchy, quick on and off, no wrestling with velcro. Loose enough at the wrist to still use a Pebble smartwatch underneath for music and notifications. No knuckle or finger coverage, but they've held up and they're only $14.
- **Stats:** Price: ~$14 | Watch: Fits under | Gap: No knuckles

### 4. JBM Elbow & Knee Pad Set
- **Link:** https://amzn.to/4gbabOz
- **Badge:** OCCASIONAL
- **Review:** Same stretchy pull-on fit, but these skip hot-weather rides — a bit bulky and I don't love the red. Haven't crashed in these specifically. For full protection I switch to a Leatt body armor set, minus the heat. A budget entry point if you're not ready for full armor.
- **Stats:** Worn: Situational | Crashed: Not yet | Runs: Bulky
